How It Works

A structured, paperless 6-step roadmap connecting classroom learning with accredited industry internships.

Step 01
👤

Create Your Account

Students register and create their profiles with academic roll numbers and college credentials.

Step 02
🎯

Build Your Profile

Add educational details, verified skills, completed course prerequisites, and certifications.

Step 03
🔍

Discover Internships

Browse open roles from verified employers and filter by role, department, and eligibility.

Step 04
⚡

Apply for Internships

Automated screening validates CGPA and prerequisite subjects before one-click submission.

Step 05
📋

Track Your Progress

Monitor your application status and interview stages in real time on the interactive Kanban board.

Step 06
🚀

Build Your Career

Complete 160 internship hours, route digital NOC e-signatures, and transfer credits directly to your gradebook.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Key Platform Features

Built to cut paperwork and keep every application visible from submit to credit.

🛡️

Automated Eligibility Screener

Instantly verifies academic requirements, minimum CGPA thresholds, and completed departmental course modules.

🏢

Verified Employer Onboarding

Vets corporate MCA 21-character CIN, 15-character GSTIN, and corporate domain emails to eliminate scam or unpaid ghost roles.

📊

Real-Time Kanban Application Tracker

Provides complete transparency on application status across Applied, Under Review, Interview, and Selection columns.

✍️

Digital Workflows & E-Signatures

Sequentially routes digital No Objection Certificates (NOC) across Mentor → HOD → Placement Officer with tamper-evident hashes.

🎓

Automated Academic Credit Transfer

Tracks 160 verified internship hours and pushes employer evaluation scores directly into institutional gradebooks for verified academic credits.

Benefits for All Stakeholders

A unified platform creating tangible advantages for students, faculty mentors, and hiring managers.

🎓

For Students

  • Internship listings you can filter by role, department, and eligibility
  • No more application black holes with live Kanban status tracking
  • A single place to update your profile, skills, and application documents
  • Seamless paperless NOC approval and guaranteed 2 academic credits
🏛️

For Colleges

  • End-to-end digital approval chain from Faculty Mentor to Department HOD
  • Automated validation of minimum CGPA and prerequisite coursework
  • Live placement analytics and cohort-level skill demand insights
  • Protection against ghost postings through strict corporate verification
💼

For Employers

  • Direct access to pre-screened students meeting exact academic criteria
  • Applicants who already meet the academic requirements for the role
  • Structured evaluation portal for submitting performance scores
  • Verified company profile badge building trust with prospective talent
