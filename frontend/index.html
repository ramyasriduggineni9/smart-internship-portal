INTELLIGENT ARCHITECTURE

Key Platform Features

Engineered to eliminate friction, manual paperwork, and application black holes.

🌐

Semantic Skill-Graph Matching

Maps skills into an interconnected knowledge graph, computing transitive affinity beyond rigid literal keyword matches.

🛡️

Automated Eligibility Screener

Instantly verifies academic requirements, minimum CGPA thresholds, and completed departmental course modules.

🏢

Verified Employer Onboarding

Vets corporate MCA 21-character CIN, 15-character GSTIN, and corporate domain emails to eliminate scam or unpaid ghost roles.

📊

Real-Time Kanban Application Tracker

Provides complete transparency on application status across Applied, Under Review, Interview, and Selection columns.

✍️

Digital Workflows & E-Signatures

Sequentially routes digital No Objection Certificates (NOC) across Mentor → HOD → Placement Officer with tamper-evident hashes.

🎓

Automated Academic Credit Transfer

Tracks 160 verified internship hours and pushes employer evaluation scores directly into institutional gradebooks for course CSE-INT.

RESEARCH FOUNDATION

IEEE Base Paper Alignment

The intelligence layer is grounded in the IEEE ICMNWC 2025 research paper: "Skill-Based Recommender System for Internship and Industry Hiring using Knowledge Graphs" (DOI: 10.1109/ICMNWC66779.2025.11354307).

While the base paper introduces graph-based skill proximity, our Smart Internship Portal extends this model into an end-to-end university ecosystem with real-world Supabase authentication, institutional NOC approval chains, and automated gradebook sync.
